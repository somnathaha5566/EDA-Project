# Hotel Booking EDA Project

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes hotel booking data from **City Hotel** and **Resort Hotel** to understand the factors driving high cancellation rates. The goal is to provide actionable business insights that help hotels reduce cancellations, optimize pricing strategies, and improve revenue efficiency.

## 📊 Dataset
- **File:** `hotel_bookings 2.csv`
- **Period Covered:** 2015–2017
- **Key Features:** hotel type, reservation status, arrival date, average daily rate (ADR), customer origin, booking channel, and cancellation status.

## 🔍 Research Questions
1. What variables affect hotel reservation cancellations?  
2. How can hotels reduce reservation cancellations?  
3. How can pricing and promotional decisions be improved?  

## 🧪 Hypotheses
- Higher prices lead to more cancellations.  
- Longer waiting lists increase cancellation likelihood.  
- Most reservations are made via offline travel agents.  

## 📈 Analysis & Findings
- **Cancellation Rate:** ~37% of reservations were canceled, significantly impacting revenue.  
- **Hotel Type:** City Hotels had more bookings, but Resort Hotels showed higher cancellation ratios.  
- **Seasonality:** August had the highest reservations and cancellations; January had the highest cancellation rate relative to bookings.  
- **Pricing Impact:** Cancellations were most common when ADR was highest.  
- **Geography:** Portugal accounted for the highest number of cancellations.  
- **Booking Channels:** 46% of clients booked via online travel agencies, while only 4% booked directly.  

## 💡 Suggestions
1. Adjust pricing strategies — offer discounts during peak cancellation periods.  
2. Provide special offers for Resort Hotels on weekends/holidays.  
3. Launch marketing campaigns in January to offset high cancellations.  
4. Improve service quality in Portugal to reduce cancellations.
